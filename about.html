About Navnit

Freelance visual designer with a cinematic motion-first style.

I create clean, engaging visuals for brands, creators, and digital projects through motion, design, editing, and visual storytelling.

I am Navnit Chaudhuri, a freelance visual designer and animation student based in Delhi, originally from Assam. I am pursuing a B.Sc. in Animation, where I continue to explore motion, storytelling, and modern digital design through both academic and freelance work.

My work focuses on clean, engaging visuals with a strong cinematic and aesthetic approach, whether it is motion graphics, edits, branding, product visuals, or creative concepts. I enjoy blending minimal design with expressive visuals to create work that feels modern, immersive, and visually striking.

I primarily work with Adobe After Effects, Premiere Pro, Illustrator, Photoshop, and DaVinci Resolve to bring ideas to life. I am deeply interested in animation, visual storytelling, creative direction, gaming, and futuristic visual styles, and I keep experimenting with new techniques to push my work further.
